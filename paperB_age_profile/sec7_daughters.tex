\section{Sons and Daughters}\label{sec:daughters}

The same layoffs that lower a son's wage in his forties raise his sister's. The contrast is significant, holds under one
rule for recording job ends, is positive in every birth cohort, and is not selection into who is observed, into work
or into marriage; it does not run through the mother's work. What it is, the data here do not settle: schooling moves
apart for sons and daughters, and daughters partner with men who earn more.
