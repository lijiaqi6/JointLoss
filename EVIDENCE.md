# Evidence ledger for the structural papers on the IGE and public policy

Papers (2026-10-17): **Paper A** = joint job loss, family insurance and the IGE (not race-focused); **Paper B** = the age
profile of the IGE and why the racial gap diverges after 35. Sections are tagged.

Every result we have, positive, null or race-neutral, with its number, source, the model ingredient it disciplines
and its policy relevance. Goal (2026-10-17): a structural paper on intergenerational mobility and public policy, with
or without a race focus. Nothing is dropped because it does not explain the racial gap.

Model roles: **T** = target moment for estimation; **E** = external (first-stage) parameter; **V** = validation /
untargeted check; **M** = motivation / framing; **D** = design choice it rules in or out.
Estimates: coefficient (s.e.); \*\*\* p<0.01, \*\* p<0.05, \* p<0.1. B = Black, W = White.
