# Joint job loss within married couples: SIPP 2004, 2008 and 2014 panels

Built by `IGE/40.SIPPJointLoss.py` (2026-10-17; definitions in its docstring). Public-use SIPP core files (2004 and 2008 panels from the NBER mirror, 2014 panel from the Census Bureau); married couples who name each other as spouse, both aged 20-60, followed month by month. **Involuntary separation (INV)** = an employer job ends in the month for layoff / slack work, closure (bankrupt, sold, plant closed), discharge, temporary job ended or other involuntary reason (reason-for-leaving item, not the CPS's unemployment reason). Probabilities are weighted by the wife's person weight; regressions are unweighted linear probability models with s.e. clustered by household. Cells: coefficient*** (s.e.; p); *** p<0.01, ** p<0.05, * p<0.1. 'Panel-month FE' = panel x rotation group x calendar month (absorbs the interview seam, which differs across rotation groups); 'state x panel-month FE' adds the state. Associations, not causal effects.
