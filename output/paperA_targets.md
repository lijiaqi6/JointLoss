## Definitions

- `cps_jl_nt` (Wife's same-month job-loss excess when he loses his, non-temporary losses (LPM, state x month FE)): Wife becomes a job loser other than on temporary layoff (unemployed with PRUNTYPE 2-3, after employment) in month t+1; husband's job loss other than on layoff (PRUNTYPE 2-3) vs husband employed; both employed at t; wives on layoff at t+1 dropped (37 Table 2, panel (i), column 3).
- `cps_jl_nt_s2` (Same, shared-employer cell (same detailed industry x detailed occupation x class of worker; s=2)): Wife becomes a job loser other than on temporary layoff (unemployed with PRUNTYPE 2-3, after employment) in month t+1; husband's job loss other than on layoff (PRUNTYPE 2-3) vs husband employed; both employed at t; wives on layoff at t+1 dropped (37 Table 2, panel (i), column 3). Spouses' main jobs at t; groups exclusive.
- `cps_jl_nt_s1` (Same, same detailed industry, outside the shared-employer cell (s=1)): Wife becomes a job loser other than on temporary layoff (unemployed with PRUNTYPE 2-3, after employment) in month t+1; husband's job loss other than on layoff (PRUNTYPE 2-3) vs husband employed; both employed at t; wives on layoff at t+1 dropped (37 Table 2, panel (i), column 3). Spouses' main jobs at t; groups exclusive.
- `cps_jl_nt_s0` (Same, different detailed industry or industry unknown (s=0)): Wife becomes a job loser other than on temporary layoff (unemployed with PRUNTYPE 2-3, after employment) in month t+1; husband's job loss other than on layoff (PRUNTYPE 2-3) vs husband employed; both employed at t; wives on layoff at t+1 dropped (37 Table 2, panel (i), column 3). Spouses' main jobs at t; groups exclusive.
- `cps_jl_nt_m3` (Wife's excess job-loss hazard in month -3 relative to his loss month, non-temporary losses): 37's Figure 1 samples (tau = his job-loss month; month -3: the wife's transition into non-temporary job loss in that month; at -3 (+3) her (his) previous month is outside the rotation, so that job loss is a new job loser by duration <= 4 weeks); husband's loss other than on layoff; wives on layoff in the outcome month dropped; state x month FE of the base month.
- `cps_jl_nt_m2` (Wife's excess job-loss hazard in month -2 relative to his loss month, non-temporary losses): 37's Figure 1 samples (tau = his job-loss month; month -2: the wife's transition into non-temporary job loss in that month; at -3 (+3) her (his) previous month is outside the rotation, so that job loss is a new job loser by duration <= 4 weeks); husband's loss other than on layoff; wives on layoff in the outcome month dropped; state x month FE of the base month.
