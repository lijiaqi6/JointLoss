# Wealth definitions: PSID couples of IGE/60's wealth targets (IGE/77.WealthDefinitions.py)

Married couples (male head, Black or White), wife 25-59 at the wealth interview; PSID wealth waves 1984, 1989, 1994, 1999-2019 (the target's waves); 43,370 couple-waves, 2,348 1968 families. 2000 $. 'Above earnings' = wealth above the couple's labor income (head + wife) of the income year before the interview (IGE/60). Unweighted. S.e. in parentheses: block bootstrap over 1968 families (200 replications); *** p<0.01, ** p<0.05, * p<0.1. Employer pensions (DB, 401(k)) are in no definition.

(a) liquid as targeted = checking/savings (+ bonds/CDs 2019) + stocks - unsecured debt (stocks include IRA stocks in 1984-1994); (b) (a) + IRAs/annuities (1999+); (c) net worth excluding home equity (PSID WEALTH1); (c') (c) minus vehicles; (d) net worth (WEALTH2).
