# Intergenerational elasticity of earnings (BFHO 2024 definition)

Columns: (1) child's log lifetime own earnings on log parental family income in one year (child 16); (2) same outcome, parental income averaged over child ages 12-17; (3) child's log lifetime family income, parental mean. Child lifetime = mean over ages 25-55 (zeros kept). Controls: child birth-year FE, quadratics in parent's age and child's mean age. S.e. clustered by family. Corrected = rho / RR (BFHO Appendix B), RR estimated in the child generation of the same data set, race by race. All = Black + White, unweighted; All, weighted = weighted by the child's sampling weight (PSID wt_pt, NLSY79 wt79, NLSY97 wt97), corrected with the unweighted pooled RR. *** p<0.01, ** p<0.05, * p<0.1.
